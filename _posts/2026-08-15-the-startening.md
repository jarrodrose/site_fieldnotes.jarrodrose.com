This the start of a place to collect my thoughts, list things I discover, note the tech discoveries I make, etc. None of this will be monumental, nor will it original in most cases. It will just be my collection of thoughts on a topic, reframing of information about project I undertake... you know, field notes.

This will also likely not be the final form.

I have started using the [Minima](https://github.com/jekyll/minima) theme for the [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) static website generator with basically no modifications.   
I will be making modifications.  
I will be shaking this around seeing what I like.

But it will give me a place to put my field notes that will make them transportable to whatever this turns into in the future.

Let the journey commence.   
(Until I forget and don't update for years... that's never happened before...)
